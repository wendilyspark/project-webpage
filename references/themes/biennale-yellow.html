00 — Context

A flagship asset with no obvious comparable

Four simultaneous constraints meant the client could not price from precedent.

CAT. 01

  • Height limits

    Policy restrictions cap buildable height, compressing the achievable unit mix.

  • Density caps

    Plot ratio ceilings limit saleable area against a very high land basis.

  • Demolition bands

    Phased clearance leaves irregular lot boundaries that resist orthogonal planning.

  • Unconventional layouts

    Irregular footprints force non-standard floorplans that UHNW buyers scrutinise.

Located in a rare prime inner-city plot with school-district advantages and elite community resources, the project faced multiple simultaneous challenges: policy restrictions on height limits, density caps, demolition bands resulting in irregular lot shapes, and unconventional unit layouts.

Site-level factors affecting ultra-luxury value perception and investment return pressures meant the client could not price from precedent. We were commissioned to conduct deep ultra-luxury market research and consumer insights — clarifying target buyer profiles, product optimisation directions, and innovative commercial model exploration.
